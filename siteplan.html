Website Plan: Growth startup power up

Site Name

Name: Growth

Reason: Growth first of stands for something which is growing so the resons of these website let people know that there is a company out there who can help grow their business faster.

Objective

The aim of this website is show case what growth company can do to help them grow their business faster and the plans we have. Which can be founded in the pricing and also testimonials from people who have worked with so that you can trust us.

Target Audience

  • Startup Businesses
  • Managers of Businesses

Key Features

  • Home Page: Toggling of Requently Asked Questions and localstorage of subscribed news letter
  • About Page: Detailed information about the company and the founder using css grid
  • Blog Page: List of blogs generated with JavaScript

Technologies and Tools

  • HTML5 and CSS3 for structuring and styling the web pages
  • JavaScript for interactive features such as side bar navigation
  • Responsive Design to ensure the site is accessible on all devices (desktops, tablets, smartphones)
